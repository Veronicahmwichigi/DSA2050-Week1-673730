## Project Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze retail sales data to identify sales patterns across products, categories, and regions, and provide insights that can support business decision-making.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### 1. Top-Selling Products
This KPI measures the total quantity of each product sold.

**Result:** Laptop sleeve was the top-selling product, with **60 units sold**.

### 2. Revenue by Category
This KPI measures the total revenue generated by each product category.

**Result:** Electronics generated the highest revenue at **KES 934,500**, followed by Personal Care at KES 931,800.

### 3. Revenue by Region
This KPI measures the total revenue generated by each region.

**Result:** Kisumu generated the highest revenue at **KES 878,050**, followed by Mombasa at KES 793,400.

## Key Findings:
    The top-selling product is the Laptop Sleeve, indicating a strong demand for this item.
    In terms of revenue, the Electronics category leads, suggesting that products in this category are driving significant sales. 
    Regionally, Kisumu generates the highest revenue, highlighting it as a critical market for the business.
    Additionally, in-store sales outperform online sales, and cash payments contribute more to revenue than card payments.
 #  These findings can inform strategic decisions regarding inventory management, marketing focus, and payment processing strategies to optimize overall business performance.
